# Explainers

Explainers cover the ideas behind more than one feature: how a figure is worked out, what it assumes, and where it stops being reliable. Each one names its assumptions, and none of them is investment advice. The limits across the whole product are on [Disclosures & Model Limits]({{ '/reference/disclosures/' | relative_url }}).

- [Price paths for multi-expiration books]({{ '/explainers/price-paths/' | relative_url }}): how Book management and the stress tester value positions whose contracts expire on different dates.
- [How Qwidgets estimates the chance of profit]({{ '/explainers/chance-of-profit/' | relative_url }}): what Probability, Win in range, and Expected Value in Trade analysis measure, and how they relate to delta.
