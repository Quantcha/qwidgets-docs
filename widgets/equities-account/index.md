# Equities Account widgets

These widgets put a brokerage account on a workspace. Each is set to one **Brokerage** and **Account** in its settings, and needs that brokerage connected; see [Brokerage accounts]({{ '/accounts/brokerage/' | relative_url }}). Widgets you open from one of them, from its **⋮** menu, use the same account.
