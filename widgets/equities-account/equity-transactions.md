# Transactions

A brokerage account's transactions, newest first. It needs a connected brokerage account; see [Brokerage accounts]({{ '/accounts/brokerage/' | relative_url }}). See [Transactions]({{ '/accounts/brokerage/transactions/' | relative_url }}).

{% include shot.html id="equity-transactions" alt="Schwab transactions: trades, an assignment, and an expiration, each with its date, type, security, quantity, and amount." %}

## What it shows

**Date**, **Type**, **Security**, **Qty**, and **Amount**, a page at a time; more load as you scroll. **Refresh** fetches new transactions from the brokerage.

## Settings

**Configure** sets the **Brokerage** and **Account**.

## Actions

**⋮** opens **Open Portfolio** and **Open Orders**.
