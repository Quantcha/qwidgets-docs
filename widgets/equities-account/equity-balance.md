# Account Balance

A brokerage account's balances. It needs a connected brokerage account; see [Brokerage accounts]({{ '/accounts/brokerage/' | relative_url }}).

{% include shot.html id="equity-balance" alt="Schwab account balances: account value, cash, margin and cash buying power, and margin requirement." %}

## What it shows

The account value in the top bar, with when it was last updated, then cash, buying power, and margin figures as the brokerage reports them.

## Settings

**Configure** sets the **Brokerage** and **Account**.

## Actions

**⋮** opens **Open Portfolio** and **Open Orders**.
