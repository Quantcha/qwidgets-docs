# Performance

How a brokerage account's campaigns have done. It needs a connected brokerage account; see [Brokerage accounts]({{ '/accounts/brokerage/' | relative_url }}). See [Performance]({{ '/accounts/brokerage/performance/' | relative_url }}) for the full page and how the figures are worked out.

{% include shot.html id="equity-performance" alt="Year-to-date performance for a Schwab account: realized profit and loss, a 50% win rate, expectancy per campaign, and a stepped equity curve." %}

## What it shows

**Realized P&L**, **Win rate**, and **Expectancy** for campaigns in the range, and the equity curve. As with the Campaign Journal, **Load all transactions** reads the account's history the first time in a session.

## Settings

**30d**, **90d**, **YTD**, **1Y**, or **All** in the top bar. **Configure** sets the **Brokerage** and **Account**.

## Actions

**⋮** opens **Open Portfolio** and **Open Orders**.
