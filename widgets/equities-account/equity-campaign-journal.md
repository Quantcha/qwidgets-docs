# Campaign Journal

A brokerage account's recent campaigns. It needs a connected brokerage account; see [Brokerage accounts]({{ '/accounts/brokerage/' | relative_url }}). See [Campaign Journal]({{ '/accounts/brokerage/campaign-journal/' | relative_url }}) for campaigns and their timelines.

{% include shot.html id="equity-campaign-journal" alt="Closed campaigns in a Schwab account: when each opened and closed, its underlying, its strategy, a tag, and its profit or loss." %}

## What it shows

Campaigns, most recent first: when each **Opened** and **Closed**, its **Underlying**, the **Campaign**'s strategy with your tags, and its **P&L**. Expand one to see its timeline and edit its note.

The first time in a session, **Load all transactions** reads the account's history, which campaigns are worked out from.

## Settings

**Closed**, **Open**, or **All** in the top bar. **Configure** sets the **Brokerage** and **Account**.

## Actions

**⋮** opens **Open Portfolio** and **Open Orders**.
