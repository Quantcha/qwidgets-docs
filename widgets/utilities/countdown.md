# Countdown

Counts down to a moment: an expiration, a release, a meeting.

{% include shot.html id="countdown" alt="A countdown titled Front-month expiration showing 20 days 18 hours 15 minutes." %}

## What it shows

Days, hours, minutes, and seconds to go, dropping units as they reach zero, and **Done!** at the end. The target is a fixed moment, so everyone who opens a shared workspace counts down to the same time.

## Settings

**Configure** sets the **Target Date & Time**, or a **Preset** from now, from 1 minute to 1 day. Give it a title so you know what it counts to.
