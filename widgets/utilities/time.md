# Time

The current time where it matters.

{% include shot.html id="time" alt="The time in Eastern US." %}

## Settings

**Configure** sets the **Time Zone**: **Local Time**, the US zones, **UTC**, **London**, **Paris**, **Tokyo**, **Shanghai**, **Kolkata**, or **Sydney**. The widget's title is the zone.
