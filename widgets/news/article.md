# Article

Feature one article or web page, with its image and summary. The whole card is the link, and opens in a new tab.

{% include shot.html id="article" alt="A featured Investor.gov page on options, with its publication, headline, and summary." %}

## Settings

**Configure** sets the **Address**; **Fill in** reads the headline, summary, and image from the page where it allows it. You can also set the **Headline**, **Summary**, **Image address**, **Publication**, **Author**, and **Published** date yourself. What you save is what everyone sees: the widget doesn't refetch the page.
