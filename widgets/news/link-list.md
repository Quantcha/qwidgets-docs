# Link List

The latest news from a feed, or a list of links you keep.

{% include shot.html id="link-list" alt="A TipRanks news feed in the detailed view: headlines with thumbnails, summaries, publisher, and age." %}

## What it shows

In **Detailed** view, each link has its image, title, description, and publisher, author, and age. **Compact** shows titles only. Links open in a new tab.

## A news feed

The latest articles from TipRanks, refreshed every few minutes. **Filters** narrows them:

- **Categories** to show or hide.
- **Tickers**: US tickers as they are, other markets with their exchange prefix, such as AU:RAD.
- **Articles**: how many, from 5 to 50.

## Your links

**Add** a link by its address; **Fill in** reads the title, description, and image from the page where it allows it. **Edit** lets you reorder, change, and remove them.

## Settings

**Configure** chooses **Your links** or **A news feed**, and the feed's **Source** and **Feed**.
