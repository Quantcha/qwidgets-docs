# Prediction Account

Your Kalshi account's value in one line. It needs a connected Kalshi account; see [Kalshi account]({{ '/accounts/kalshi/' | relative_url }}).

{% include shot.html id="event-contract-account" alt="A Kalshi account with its cash, positions, and total value." %}

## What it shows

**Cash**, **Positions**, and **Total**.

## Settings

**Configure** sets the **Integration**: your Kalshi account.

## Actions

**⋮** opens **Open Portfolio**, **Open Orders**, and **Open Settlements**.
