# Prediction Settlements

Markets you held that have resolved, and how they paid. It needs a connected Kalshi account; see [Kalshi account]({{ '/accounts/kalshi/' | relative_url }}).

## What it shows

A row per settlement: the **Market**, your **Side** (marked won or lost), **Quantity**, **Total Cost**, **Fees**, **Revenue**, and when it **Settled**. **Load More** reaches further back.

## Settings

**Configure** sets the **Integration**: your Kalshi account.

## Actions

**⋮** opens **Open Account** and **Open Orders**.
