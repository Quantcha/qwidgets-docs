# Market Chart

One market's chance, at a glance.

{% include shot.html id="event-contract-market" alt="A gauge showing a Kalshi inflation market at its current Yes probability, with the bid and ask in the top bar." %}

## What it shows

A gauge of the market's current probability, with the live bid and ask and the expected settlement in the top bar.

## Settings

**Yes** or **No** in the top bar chooses the side shown. **Configure** sets the **Provider** and **Market ID**.

## Actions

**⋮** opens the market's event (**Open Event Chart**, **Open Event History**, **Open Event Table**), **Open Market Candles**, and **Open Order Book**, and **Trade via** your Kalshi account.
