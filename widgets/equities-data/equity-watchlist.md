# Watchlist

Quote several symbols at once. It works on the delayed feed with no account, and on real-time data from a connected brokerage. Choose the source as the **Provider** in the widget's settings.

{% include shot.html id="equity-watchlist" alt="A watchlist of SPY, QQQ, AAPL, MSFT, and NVDA, each with an intraday sparkline, last price, and change." %}

## What it shows

A row per symbol: its name, an intraday sparkline, the last price, and the change.

## Settings

- **+ Add** adds a symbol; **Edit** shows a remove button on each row.
- **List**, **Symbol**, **Gain**, or **Loss** sorts the rows.
- **Configure** sets the **Provider**.

## Actions

Each row's **⋮** opens **Open Quote**, **Open Price Chart**, **Open Option Chain**, and **Open Option Chart** for its symbol.
