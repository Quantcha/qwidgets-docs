# Market Clock

Counts down to the US stock market's next open or close, allowing for holidays and early closes. It needs no data source and no settings.

{% include shot.html id="equity-market-clock" alt="Market Open, with 5 hours 44 minutes until the 4:00 PM Eastern close." %}

{% include shot.html id="equity-market-clock-closed" alt="Market Closed on a weekend, with 1 day 23 hours until Monday 9:30 AM Eastern." %}

## What it shows

**Market Open** or **Market Closed**, the time until the next change, and, when the widget is tall enough, why and when: "early close", "weekend", "holiday", "pre-market", or "after hours", then the close or open time in Eastern time. It follows the regular session, 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM Eastern.
