# Stock

A quote for one stock or ETF, with its session detail. It works on the delayed feed with no account, and on real-time data from a connected brokerage. Choose the source as the **Provider** in the widget's settings.

{% include shot.html id="equity-stock" alt="A SPY quote from Schwab: last price and change, bid, ask, open, previous close, volume, the session range, and the next ex-dividend date." %}

## What it shows

The last price and change in the top bar, then **Bid**, **Ask**, **Open**, **Prev close**, **Volume**, and **Prev volume**, the **Session range** with where the price sits in it, and upcoming earnings and ex-dividend dates. A spread too wide to trade on is grayed out.

## Settings

**Configure** sets the **Provider** and **Symbol**.

## Actions

**⋮** opens **Open Price Chart** and, for optionable symbols, **Open Option Chain** and **Open Option Chart**.
