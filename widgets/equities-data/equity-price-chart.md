# Price Chart

A stock's price as candles, from today's session to its full history. It works on the delayed feed with no account, and on real-time data from a connected brokerage. Choose the source as the **Provider** in the widget's settings. It's the chart on the [stock page]({{ '/equities/stocks/' | relative_url }}).

{% include shot.html id="equity-price-chart" alt="A one-month daily candlestick chart of SPY from Schwab, with range buttons from 1D to Max in the top bar." %}

## Settings

The range buttons in the top bar, **1D** through **Max**, set the span and are saved with the widget. Sources offer different history, so some ranges may be missing. **Configure** sets the **Provider** and **Symbol**.

## Actions

**⋮** opens **Open Quote**, **Open Option Chain**, and **Open Option Chart**.
