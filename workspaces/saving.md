# Saving

{% include needs.html tier="account" scope="page" %}

Without an account, you have the [guest workspace]({{ '/workspaces/' | relative_url }}#kinds-of-workspaces), which lasts as long as its browser tab.

## What saves automatically

Everything you do on the canvas saves as you do it: adding, moving, resizing, configuring, cloning, and removing widgets, and the zoom level. There's no save button.

A workspace's name and description are the exception; you change those on its manage page.

## My Workspaces

**My Workspaces**, in the left-hand menu, lists your workspaces with favorites first.

- **Create** starts a new, empty workspace. Give it a **Name** and **Description**, check **Favorite** if you like, and select **Create Workspace**.
- **Filter** the list by name or description.
- The **star** on a workspace's card makes it a favorite. Favorites appear on the home page and in the left-hand menu.
- **Open** goes to the workspace. The gear opens its manage page. The trash can deletes it, after you confirm.

## Rename or describe a workspace

On the workspace's manage page (the gear on its card), change **Name**, **Description**, or **Favorite**, then select **Update Workspace**. The same page controls [sharing]({{ '/workspaces/sharing/' | relative_url }}).
