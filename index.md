# Qwidgets Docs

**A modern investment platform for active traders.** One place to plan, track, and manage investments of any kind.

These docs explain what each part of Qwidgets does and how to use it. Qwidgets is free, and most of it works without an account.

[Open Qwidgets]({{ site.app_url }}){: .btn .btn-primary target="_blank" rel="noopener" }
[Getting Started]({{ '/getting-started/' | relative_url }}){: .btn }

## Plan

Know what a trade does before you place it, and find trades you wouldn't build by hand.

- [Options]({{ '/options/' | relative_url }}): chains with Greeks, trade analysis, option charting, option and calendar search, and the trade screener.
- [Equities]({{ '/equities/' | relative_url }}): quotes, price history, and the stock screener.
- [Prediction markets]({{ '/prediction-markets/' | relative_url }}): event contract prices as another read on the events you already have a view on.

## Track

Watch everything in one place, and see how each position went.

- [Workspaces]({{ '/workspaces/' | relative_url }}): compose widgets into a view you return to, and share it by link.
- [Accounts and connections]({{ '/accounts/' | relative_url }}): connect Schwab, E\*TRADE, and more for your own positions, orders, and performance.

## Manage

Manage a position across its life, and see your account's risk.

- [Book management]({{ '/options/book-management/' | relative_url }}): what one underlying's positions are worth now and at a target date.
- [Stress testing]({{ '/options/stress-testing/' | relative_url }}): reprice the whole account under the prices and volatility you choose.
