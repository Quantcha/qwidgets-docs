# Equities

**Stocks & Options**, in the left-hand menu, is where you look up any US stock or ETF. Everything here works without an account, on the delayed feed. Connect a brokerage for fresher quotes and to trade.

{% include shot.html id="equities-hub" alt="The Stocks & Options page with a symbol search box, Tools cards for the Trade Screener and Stock Screener, links to every tool for a symbol, and popular symbols." %}

## The Stocks & Options page

- **Search for a symbol or company.** Pick a suggestion to open that symbol's page. If what you typed isn't found, **Use … as typed** tries it anyway.
- **Tools:** the [Trade Screener]({{ '/options/trade-screener/' | relative_url }}) and the [Stock Screener]({{ '/equities/stock-screener/' | relative_url }}).
- **On every symbol:** shortcuts to each tool a symbol page has, and a few popular symbols.
- **Brokerage:** **Connect a brokerage**, or open the ones you've connected.
- **Workspaces:** the stock and option widgets you can put on a workspace, and a link to try one.

## In this section

- [Stocks and quotes]({{ '/equities/stocks/' | relative_url }}): a symbol's page, its price chart, quote, earnings and dividend dates, and where its data comes from.
- [Stock screener]({{ '/equities/stock-screener/' | relative_url }}): filter and rank the whole US stock market.

A symbol's option tools are under [Options]({{ '/options/' | relative_url }}). Your brokerage accounts are under [Accounts and connections]({{ '/accounts/brokerage/' | relative_url }}).
