# Prediction markets

Prediction markets price real-world outcomes, such as a rate decision or an election, as probabilities: a contract trading at 62 cents implies a 62% chance. Qwidgets brings events from Kalshi, Polymarket, and more into one place, so you can read them beside the rest of your work: another view on the events you already have an opinion about. No account is needed to browse.

{% include shot.html id="predictions-hub" alt="The Prediction Markets page: a search across exchanges, the most-traded events of the last 24 hours, and a card for each exchange." %}

## The Prediction Markets page

**Providers**, under **Predictions** in the left-hand menu, opens it:

- **Search across exchanges:** one search over every exchange's events.
- **Trending:** the most-traded events across every exchange in the last 24 hours.
- **Browse by exchange:** each exchange's own page. Kalshi is a US exchange regulated by the CFTC; Polymarket settles in USDC; Manifold runs play-money markets anyone can create; PredictIt runs US political markets with a cap on the size of any one position.
- **Featured workspaces** built around what's moving now.
