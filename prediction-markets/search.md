# Prediction search

## Across every exchange

**Search across exchanges**, on the [Prediction Markets page]({{ '/prediction-markets/' | relative_url }}), searches every exchange at once. Enter your terms and select **Search**. Each result shows the event, its type, its competition and scope where it has them, when it's expected to settle, and its 24-hour volume. **View Event Details** opens it.

## On one exchange

Each exchange's page, such as **Kalshi Prediction Markets**, searches that exchange only:

- **Search:** suggestions appear after two letters, and results update as you type.
- **Type:** limit results to one kind of event, such as a single market, a ladder of thresholds, a set of ranges, or several outcomes.
- **Sort Order:** by relevance, by 24-hour volume, or by expected settlement.
- **Card view** or **Table view.** The table lists title, type, settlement, and 24-hour volume.

Search covers events that are still open on each exchange.

## On a workspace

The [Prediction Search widget]({{ '/widgets/event-contract-search/' | relative_url }}) puts the same search on a workspace, across every exchange or one of them.
