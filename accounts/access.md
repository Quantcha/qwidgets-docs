# Access and sign-in

Qwidgets has three levels of access. Each adds to the one before.

## Guest

Without signing in you can use most of Qwidgets: stock and option data and every options analysis tool, prediction market data from every exchange, AI compute, shared workspaces, and the guest workspace.

Nothing is kept between sessions. The guest workspace lasts as long as the browser tab you built it in.

## Signed in

A free Qwidgets account adds what Qwidgets keeps for you:

- Workspaces saved to your account, and available on any device you sign in on.
- Favorite workspaces on the home page and in the left-hand menu.
- Copies of shared workspaces, with **Copy To My Workspaces**.
- Saved trades, searches, and screens.

**Register** in the top bar creates an account; **Login** signs you in. You can sign in with your email or user name and password, with a link sent to your email, or with an external account.

## Connected accounts

Each connection adds what its kind of account provides:

- **A brokerage** (Schwab, E\*TRADE, and more) adds your positions, balances, and orders, trading from Qwidgets, transaction history with performance tracking and a campaign journal, and quotes that refresh about every 15 seconds. See [Brokerage accounts]({{ '/accounts/brokerage/' | relative_url }}).
- **Kalshi** adds your event contract portfolio, orders, settlements, and trading. See [Kalshi account]({{ '/accounts/kalshi/' | relative_url }}).

Book management, stress testing, and performance tracking all work from your positions, so they need a connected brokerage.

Connections are yours alone. They're never shared, not even in a workspace you share by link. See [Sharing by link]({{ '/workspaces/sharing/' | relative_url }}#what-people-see).
