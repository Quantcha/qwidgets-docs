# Tutorials

Each tutorial walks through one task from start to finish, with a screenshot at each step and a short question at the end.

- [Stress test a multi-expiration book]({{ '/tutorials/stress-test-multi-expiration-book/' | relative_url }}): value a calendar spread past its short leg's expiration, then see what a sharp fall would do to it.
- [Analyze a covered call]({{ '/tutorials/analyze-a-covered-call/' | relative_url }}): add a short call to 100 shares in Trade analysis, value it at expiration, and read what the premium pays and what it caps.
- [Screen for covered calls and cash-secured puts]({{ '/tutorials/screen-for-covered-calls/' | relative_url }}): search the whole market for calls in the delta and expiration range you want, without earnings or a dividend before expiration, then run the same screen for puts.
- [Analyze a cash-secured put]({{ '/tutorials/analyze-a-secured-put/' | relative_url }}): build one in Trade analysis, widen the forecast to read its chance of profit, and see what a closer strike changes.
- [Follow a Wheel in the Campaign Journal]({{ '/tutorials/follow-a-wheel-campaign/' | relative_url }}): see a Wheel's put, assignment, and covered call as one campaign, and review all your Wheels together.
